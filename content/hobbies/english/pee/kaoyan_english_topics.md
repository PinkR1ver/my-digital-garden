## 1. Culture & Tradition

### Key Vocabulary
- **traditional culture**
- **cultural heritage**
- **carry forward / inherit**
- **cultural exchange**
- **integration of cultures**
- **essence and dross**

### Viewpoint Sentences
- **Preservation**: Traditional culture is the root of a nation's spirit and should be cherished and preserved.
- **Innovation**: We should not only inherit traditional culture but also innovate it to adapt to modern society.
- **Exchange**: Cultural exchange promotes mutual understanding and global peace.
- **Attitude**: We should "discard the dross and select the essence" when dealing with traditional culture.

## 2. Technology & Internet

### Key Vocabulary
- **artificial intelligence (AI)**
- **information age**
- **double-edged sword**
- **convenience and efficiency**
- **addiction / indulgence**
- **cyber security**

### Viewpoint Sentences
- **Impact**: Technology has dramatically changed the way we live, work, and communicate.
- **Pros & Cons**: While the internet brings convenience, we must be alert to its negative effects, such as cybercrime and addiction.
- **Balance**: We should be the masters of technology, not its slaves.
- **Future**: AI should be developed responsibly to serve humanity rather than replace it.

## 3. Environment & Protection

### Key Vocabulary
- **sustainable development**
- **environmental protection**
- **eco-friendly**
- **pollution / contamination**
- **low-carbon lifestyle**
- **ecological balance**

### Viewpoint Sentences
- **Economy vs Environment**: Economic growth should not be achieved at the expense of the environment.
- **Responsibility**: Protecting the environment is protecting ourselves; we share one planet.
- **Action**: Adopting a low-carbon lifestyle is an effective way for individuals to contribute to environmental protection.
- **Urgency**: It is high time that we took effective measures to solve environmental problems.

## 4. Education & Personal Growth

### Key Vocabulary
- **lifelong learning**
- **quality education**
- **critical thinking**
- **innovation ability**
- **all-round development**
- **psychological health**

### Viewpoint Sentences
- **Purpose**: Education is not just about acquiring knowledge but also about cultivating character and ability.
- **Necessity**: In this rapidly changing world, lifelong learning is essential for personal and professional success.
- **Qualities**: Persistence and optimism are key qualities that help us overcome difficulties on the road to success.
- **Mindset**: Failure is the mother of success; we learn more from our mistakes than from our achievements.

## 5. Social Ethics

### Key Vocabulary
- **social morality**
- **integrity / honesty**
- **cooperation and competition**
- **responsibility / obligation**
- **harmonious society**
- **mutual trust**

### Viewpoint Sentences
- **Honesty**: Honesty is the best policy and the foundation of a harmonious society.
- **Cooperation**: Cooperation is as important as competition; win-win results are the ultimate goal.
- **Civic Duty**: Every citizen has the responsibility to abide by laws and social norms.
- **Virtue**: Helping others is a traditional virtue that should be advocated in modern society.

## 6. Youth & Dreams

### Key Vocabulary
- **pursue dreams**
- **down-to-earth**
- **ambition / aspiration**
- **seize the opportunity**
- **shoulder the responsibility**

### Viewpoint Sentences
- **Dream**: A dream gives us direction and motivation to move forward.
- **Action**: Dreams will not come true unless we put them into action.
- **Resilience**: Young people should be brave enough to face challenges and never give up easily.

## 7. Health & Lifestyle

### Key Vocabulary
- **physical and mental health**
- **sub-health status**
- **sedentary lifestyle**
- **balanced diet**
- **relieve stress**
- **work-life balance**

### Viewpoint Sentences
- **Priority**: Health is wealth; nothing is more important than a healthy body and a sound mind.
- **Prevention**: Prevention is better than cure; a regular lifestyle is key to maintaining health.
- **Exercise**: Physical exercise not only strengthens our body but also helps to relieve stress and improve mood.
- **Balance**: Maintaining a balance between work and rest is essential for long-term productivity and happiness.

## 8. Reading & Knowledge

### Key Vocabulary
- **fragmented reading**
- **information explosion**
- **spiritual enrichment**
- **broaden one's horizon**
- **critical reading**
- **digital vs. traditional reading**

### Viewpoint Sentences
- **Value**: Books are the ladder of human progress; reading enriches our minds and broadens our horizons.
- **Deep Reading**: In the age of information fragmentation, we should cultivate the habit of deep reading to gain systematic knowledge.
- **Classic**: Classic literature stands the test of time and offers wisdom that is applicable to modern life.
- **Method**: We should apply what we learn from books to practice, as knowledge without practice is useless.

## 9. Family & Relationships

### Key Vocabulary
- **aging society**
- **filial piety**
- **generation gap**
- **empty-nesters**
- **mutual understanding**
- **companionship**

### Viewpoint Sentences
- **Tradition**: Respecting the elderly and caring for the young are traditional virtues of the Chinese nation.
- **Communication**: Effective communication is the bridge to narrow the generation gap and build harmonious family relationships.
- **Company**: Companionship is the best gift we can give to our parents and family members.
- **Responsibility**: As society ages, caring for the elderly is not only a family responsibility but also a social obligation.

## 10. Youth Issues (Juvenile Delinquency)

### Key Vocabulary
- **juvenile delinquency**
- **go astray**
- **improper upbringing**
- **lack of supervision**
- **school bullying**
- **legal awareness**

### Viewpoint Sentences
- **Causes**: Lack of parental supervision and improper family education are often the root causes of juvenile delinquency.
- **Environment**: A healthy social and campus environment is crucial for the growth of teenagers.
- **Solution**: Solving this problem requires the joint efforts of families, schools, and society to provide better guidance.
- **Education**: Legal education should be strengthened to enhance teenagers' awareness of the law.

## 11. Integrity & Consumer Rights

### Key Vocabulary
- **academic misconduct / plagiarism**
- **fake and shoddy products**
- **food safety**
- **legitimate rights and interests**
- **crack down on**
- **moral decline**

### Viewpoint Sentences
- **Trust**: Trust is hard to build but easy to destroy; regaining public trust is a long-term process.
- **Regulation**: The government should improve relevant laws and regulations to punish those who violate market rules.
- **Academic Integrity**: Honesty is the baseline of academic research; any form of plagiarism must be strictly prohibited.
- **Consequence**: Producing fake products not only harms consumers' rights but also damages the reputation of the company.
