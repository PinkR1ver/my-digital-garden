# Category

* Opinion Essay
	* Example:
		* Space Exploration is much too expensive, and the money should be spent on more important things. What is your opinion
* Advantage and Disadvantage Essay
	* Example:
		* Some people believe that is better to take a gap year before going to university, while others think that this can be a waste of time and that going straight into higher education is the best option. Discuss both views and give your opinion
* Problem Essay
	* Example:
		* Despite a large number of gyms, a sedentary lifestyle is gaining popularity in the contemporary world. What problems are associated with this? What solutions can you suggest?


# Understanding the Topic of the Essay


Three ways the essay can be presented in the exam

* Type 1: Two opposite views to discuss;
	* Some people think that more money should be invested into space exploration as it is a vital form of investigation for the future of humanity, while others believe it is a waste of vital funding which could otherwise be used towards more important projects here on earth.
* Type 2: Two opposite views to discuss using the word *should* in the instruction
	* Should more money be invested into space exploration or should it be used towards more important projects here on earth?
* Type 3: A statement to discuss
	* Paying for space exploration is a waste of vital funding which could otherwise be used towards more important projects on earth.

# The structure of Paragraphs

* A sentence that introduces the topic or gist of the paragraph (and can link it to the previous paragraph)
* 2-3 sentences that develop the topic (with analysis, evidence or detail)
* 1 sentence that concludes the topic (or links it to the next paragraph).

# Showing Balance in Essay

Always remain modest about opinion and show reader that you understand that you may be wrong just like anybody else.

For example, "**Students** have a very low level of basic mathematical knowledge due to over-dependence on calculators" is not good, we should say "Over-dependence on calculators **may** have a negative effect on the basic mathematical knowledge of **some students**"

Use cautious language, also called hedging language.

Also remember, don't use cautious language in an inappropriate way.

# Being Specific enough

Don't use thing in essay, be specific.

# Link your sentence in IELTS Writing

* in order to, so as to
* such as, for example, for instance
* receive, acquire, obtain
* aid, assist, support
* Not only ..., but also
