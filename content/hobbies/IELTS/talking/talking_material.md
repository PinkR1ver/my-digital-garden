## A Planet

One of my favorite plants is Sakura, or cherry blossom, which holds significant cultural value in Japan. These trees bloom in spring, **producing delicate pink and white flowers that create breathtaking landscapes**. The fleeting nature of their bloom symbolizes the transient beauty of life.

## A person's Appearance

I'd like to describe my mentor, Dr. Ning, who has a distinguished appearance. He always wears **well-tailored suits, complemented by polished shoes and a neatly tied tie**. His hair is **meticulously groomed**, and he often sports a **subtle, confident smile**. His professional attire reflects his dedication and the respect he commands in his field. Beyond his appearance, his demeanor is calm and approachable, making him an inspiring figure in my academic journey.
