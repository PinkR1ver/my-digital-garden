# Part 2 Template

明白了！你需要的是 **3个具有波折结构的口语模板**，分别对应：

1. **已发生的事（已做的体验）**
    
2. **未发生的事（想做、计划中、假设的事）**
    
3. **和某人有关的事（描述人物+关系+经历）**
    

我将为你写出每个模板的**通用结构**，并加上一些**信号词和典型句型**，以方便你将前面那些素材轻松代入。每个模板都故意设计得稍微“波折”一些，避免平铺直叙，让你的叙述更具节奏感和故事性。

---

## ✅ 模板一：**已有的事（回忆+过程+转折+收获）**

适用话题：书籍、电影、技能、运动、室内/室外经历等。

### 📎结构：

1. **起点：我没预料会对这个感兴趣**
2. **过程：怎么开始的？有什么挑战？**
3. **转折点/特别的瞬间：改变了我的看法或感受**
4. **结尾：我学到了什么，是否会继续**
    

### 📘范句：

- _To be honest, I didn’t expect much at first..._
- _But the more I got into it, the more I realized..._
- _At one point, something really unexpected happened..._
- _Looking back, it was one of the most... experiences I've had._
    

### 📌示例应用（运动：轻量化户外徒步）：

> I didn’t expect myself to enjoy hiking that much. A friend invited me to try ultralight hiking, and at first I thought it would be exhausting and uncomfortable. But as we climbed into the mountains with barely 6kg of gear, I realized how freeing it was to carry only what you really need. One night, cooking under the stars with nothing but silence around us, I felt more connected to nature than ever. It totally changed how I think about travel and comfort.

---

## ✅ 模板二：**未有的事（好奇+犹豫+渴望+计划）**

适用话题：技能、户外、名人、未来想读的书、想送的礼物等。

### 📎结构：

1. **起点：我一直对它感兴趣但没尝试过**
2. **障碍/原因：为什么还没做（没时间、没机会、觉得难）**
3. **幻想/场景构想：如果做了会怎样？**
4. **结尾：我希望什么时候去做，它对我有什么意义**
    

### 📘范句：

- _It’s something I’ve always been curious about, but..._
- _Part of the reason I haven’t done it yet is..._
- _I often imagine what it would be like if..._
- _Hopefully, I’ll get a chance to try it in the near future._
    

### 📌示例应用（技能：vibe coding）：

> I've always been fascinated by people who can just open their laptops and start coding something from scratch — not for work, but just for the vibe. I haven’t really done it myself because I tend to over-plan things, and coding without a goal feels a bit strange to me. But I imagine sitting in a café with headphones on, just creating something spontaneously, would feel incredibly freeing. I’m planning to try this style during my next break, and hopefully it’ll help me reconnect with the fun side of coding.

## ✅ 模板三：**和人相关（关系+事件+变化+印象）**

适用话题：名人、收到的礼物、和朋友/亲人相关的经历等。

### 📎结构：

1. **起点：我和这个人有关（认识、仰慕、被影响）**
2. **事件：有一件事让我对他/她印象特别深**
3. **波折：起初我并不理解/赞同/在意**
4. **结尾：这件事改变了我对他的看法 / 给我启发**
    

### 📘范句：

- _I’ve known this person for a while / admired them for years..._
- _There was one moment when I truly saw a different side of them..._
- _At first, I wasn’t sure what to think, but then..._
- _Now, I see them as someone who really..._
    

### 📌示例应用（名人：Brad Pitt）：

> I’ve always seen Brad Pitt as just another Hollywood superstar — famous, good-looking, but not particularly deep. But after watching some behind-the-scenes interviews and reading about his charity work and love for architecture, I started to see a different side of him. One interview where he spoke about addiction and masculinity really stuck with me — it was raw and honest. That changed my impression completely. Now, I admire him not just as an actor but as a human being who's constantly evolving.

## 🎯 模板一：已有的事（书籍：《一首小夜曲》 by 伊坂幸太郎）

**题目示例**：Describe a book you have read more than once

> To be honest, I picked up _A Night Music_ by Kotaro Isaka without much expectation. I’d never read anything by him before, and the title sounded a bit vague to me. But once I started reading, I was immediately drawn into his world — a quiet, almost dreamlike version of urban Japan where strange things happen to ordinary people.
> 
> What really surprised me was how the short stories, which seemed totally separate at first, slowly began to connect in subtle ways. One character who appeared briefly in one story suddenly became central in another, and that gave me this strange feeling of everything being part of a bigger picture.
> 
> There was one story about a man who kept receiving mysterious notes in his mailbox that honestly gave me chills. But by the end, it made me reflect on how we interpret loneliness and connection.
> 
> I ended up reading it again a few months later, and it felt completely different — deeper, even warmer in a way. It’s definitely a book I’d recommend, especially if you enjoy stories that quietly mess with your head.

## 🎯 模板二：未有的事（技能：vibe coding）

**题目示例**：Describe a skill you’d like to learn in the future

> I’ve always been interested in coding, but lately I’ve become fascinated with something more specific — what some people call “vibe coding.” Basically, it’s when you just sit down, maybe put on some music, and start building something from scratch, purely based on how you feel in that moment.
> 
> I haven’t actually tried it yet. I tend to overthink when I code — planning everything, worrying about structure, making sure it's useful. That mindset makes it hard to just relax and create. But I often imagine sitting in a cozy café, laptop open, just typing away with no particular goal — maybe making a small game, a generative artwork, or some weird little website.
> 
> I think the main reason I haven’t started is because I’ve always associated coding with work or study. Doing it for fun feels unfamiliar. But I know that once I break that barrier, it might become a totally different and exciting experience.
> 
> Hopefully during my next holiday, I’ll get to try this. Not for productivity, just to enjoy the process. I think it’ll be good for both my creativity and my peace of mind.

## 🎯 模板三：和人相关（名人：Brad Pitt）

**题目示例**：Describe a famous person you admire

> For a long time, I thought Brad Pitt was just another good-looking movie star. I liked some of his movies, like _Fight Club_, but I didn’t think much beyond that. He seemed cool, stylish, but a little too perfect, you know?
> 
> But that changed when I watched an interview where he opened up about some personal struggles — addiction, divorce, and aging in Hollywood. He was surprisingly open, even vulnerable. That really changed how I saw him.
> 
> Later, I found out he’s also a serious architecture enthusiast. He even started a housing project to help rebuild New Orleans after Hurricane Katrina. I never imagined he’d care so much about design and social responsibility.
> 
> Now, I admire him not just for his acting skills, but for being someone who’s constantly evolving, dealing with personal issues while still trying to give back to the world. He’s more than a celebrity — he’s someone who’s trying to grow, just like the rest of us.
